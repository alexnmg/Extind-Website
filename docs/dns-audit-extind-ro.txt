## ============================================================
## TARGET ZONE — what to create in the client's new account
## ============================================================
##
## !! DO NOT BULK-IMPORT THE BIND FILE. !!
## It would recreate `A extind.ro -> 157.90.32.237`, i.e. the OLD site, and
## CNAMEs for www/ftp — which then collide with the Pages custom domain.
## Create these seven records by hand instead.

MX     @                    1 smtp.google.com                       (Auto TTL)
TXT    @                    v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com +a +ip4:157.90.32.237 ~all
TXT    @                    google-site-verification=zfXc_0NN_6sMvWn_XYVvghSM-mtxGe-LCzYe0k3YGFY
TXT    @                    v=DMARC1; p=none;                       (carry as-is, see runbook)
TXT    default._domainkey   <full key below>
TXT    google._domainkey    <full key below>
A      mail                 157.90.32.237      *** DNS-only / grey cloud ***

Then let Cloudflare Pages create the apex and www records itself when the
custom domains are added. Do not pre-create them.

DROP: ftp.extind.ro. It is a proxied CNAME to the website, and FTP does not
work through an HTTP proxy — it has never done anything. If you would rather
keep it byte-identical, recreate it as a proxied CNAME to @ after cutover.
